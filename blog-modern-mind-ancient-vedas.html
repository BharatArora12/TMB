“Does knowledge from a thousand years ago still matter in the world of AI and Social Media?”
— A Question for the Modern Mind

A few days ago, I heard news about Vedmurti Devvrat Mahesh Rekhe, a young Vedic scholar from Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra.

At the young age of 19, he accomplished something amazing by completing the Dandakrama Parayanam, one of the most challenging forms of Vedic recitation.

He recited nearly 2,000 mantras from the Shukla Yajurveda's Madhyandina branch over 50 uninterrupted days in Kashi.

Now let us take a pause and think about an age where most of us are busy scrolling on social media, busy in our careers, looking for shortcuts with AI. “How much dedication, concentration, and consistency does it require for Vedmurti Devvrat to complete Dandakrama Parayanam?” From a young age, he dedicated his years of life to learning an ancient tradition that demands exceptional concentration, discipline, and memory.
