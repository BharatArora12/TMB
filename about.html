FOUNDER'S CONVICTION

From a single spark of conviction to a national movement.

Witnessing a rising generation disconnected from their civilizational roots and conditioned by passive narratives, our founder stood up with a fierce resolve: to build an uncompromising platform where India's youth can reclaim their intellectual sovereignty. Starting with zero institutional backing, no commercial shortcuts, and sheer grassroots grit, Towards My Bharat turned raw passion into a thriving national movement empowering thousands across campuses and cities to lead with courage.
